  4. Get tattooed

    Relax in a clean studio, then leave with clear aftercare instructions.

Aftercare

  1. Keep it covered

    Leave the wrap your artist applied on for as long as they advise, then remove it gently.

  2. Wash gently

    Use lukewarm water and a mild, fragrance-free soap. Pat dry with a clean paper towel. Never rub.

  3. Moisturize lightly

    Apply a thin layer of the ointment or lotion your artist recommends. The skin should not feel suffocated.

  4. Hands off

    Do not pick or scratch peeling skin. Avoid soaking, pools, the sea and direct sun until fully healed.

  5. Ask us

    Redness or swelling that keeps getting worse needs medical attention. For anything else, message us.

FAQs

Where are you located?

Corner Street Blk 3, Tarlac City, Philippines. You can reach us at (+63) 999 123 4567 or inksharpstudio@gmail.com.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We recommend booking first so your preferred artist and time are secured. Walk-ins are only possible when an artist has an open slot.

How do I book an appointment?

Press Schedule an Appointment and tell us your idea, size, placement, preferred artist and date. We will reply to confirm your slot.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Price depends on size, detail, placement and whether the piece has color. We give you a quote after we review your idea.

How long does a session take?

Small pieces can take an hour or two. Large or highly detailed work may be split across several sessions.

How should I prepare?

Eat beforehand, drink water, get enough sleep, wear comfortable clothes and avoid alcohol for 24 hours before your session.

Can I bring my own reference?

Yes. Your artist will turn it into an original design and adjust it to fit your body.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes, after a consultation to look at the existing tattoo. Some designs need to be larger or darker to cover well.

How long does healing take?

The surface usually heals in two to four weeks. The skin keeps settling for a couple of months after that.

When can I swim or sunbathe?

Wait until the tattoo is fully healed. After that, sunscreen keeps the ink looking fresh.
