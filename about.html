About Us

The People Behind the Ink

INK SHARP is a small, artist-run tattoo studio built on custom work, honest advice and a clean, calm space.

Our Story

INK SHARP started with three artists who shared a simple idea: every tattoo should be drawn for the person wearing it. Instead of picking flash off a wall, our clients sit down with an artist, talk through the meaning behind the piece and leave with a design that belongs to them alone.

Each of us came up through a different style — realism, traditional and fine line — and the studio grew around that mix. Today we work side by side, trade ideas and hold every piece to the same standard: clean lines, smooth shading and a tattoo that still looks sharp years after it heals.

Our Mission

We are driven by a steady pursuit of craft. Our promise is to make custom tattoos that are original, detailed and meaningful, and to treat every client with care from the first sketch to the final healed result.
